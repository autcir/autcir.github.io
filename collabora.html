english

AutCir is an Italian YouTube channel where I test AI tools and automation on real work, with audio tracks and subtitles in English, German, French, Spanish and Polish. Audience: Italian professionals, developers and small businesses. Formats: a 30–60 s sponsored segment inside the daily "I really try the tool" video, a dedicated review (software or hardware: laptops, monitors, keyboards, mini PCs), a Short, or a community post. Rules: we use every product on real work first, we say what does not work, and every deal is disclosed ("includes paid promotion"). The numbers above are real and updated every week; the channel is new and growing daily.
