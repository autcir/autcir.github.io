Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. Applies to AutCir Studio (Google Cloud project "autcir-youtube") and to "AutCir Drafts", its TikTok app.

Who uses it

AutCir Studio is used only by the owner of the AutCir YouTube channel, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, it uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to upload, schedule and update the owner's own videos, playlists, captions and thumbnails, and to read the owner's own channel statistics.

TikTok

With the owner's consent through TikTok Login Kit, "AutCir Drafts" uses the TikTok Content Posting API (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload) to send the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok drafts. The owner then edits and posts them in the TikTok app. It reads no other data. Access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services.

How data is stored and shared

Authorization tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent to any server other than Google's and TikTok's APIs. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local tokens are then deleted by the owner.
