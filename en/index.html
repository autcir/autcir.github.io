about me

I'm Ciro Autuori, founder of StudioCentOS, between Salerno and Barcelona. I build sovereign software: your server, your data, your code. Most days that means a Rust agent engine, a few Linux machines tuned by hand, and AI agents doing the parts I would rather not type twice.

AutCir is my public test bench. What works, we also use for clients; what doesn't, I say so. When there is gaming, the AI plays.

er0s: a sovereign agent engine in Rust. A deterministic kernel does the math without model calls; six agent roles (CEO, CFO, CTO, COO, CBO, CLO) share one Brain; a human approval gate sits in front of anything that matters. Multi-tenant, fail-closed, delivered on your own European server.
